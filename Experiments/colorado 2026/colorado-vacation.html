🛏️ Tonight: Dillon Airbnb · Last night!

    Free Summit Stage shuttle: Frisco → Breckenridge
    No parking stress — take the free bus!

    BreckConnect Gondola ride (Free!)
    Stunning views of the ski resort and valley · Ride up, explore, ride back down
  • 🚲
    Blue River Bikeway — Breckenridge to Frisco
    9 miles one-way · Fully paved, no highway traffic · 525 ft gentle downhill — a cruise for kids! · 1–2 hrs for kids, under 3 hrs with breaks

    • Leave bikes with their pickup service in Frisco
    • Optional add-on: Dillon Reservoir Lakeshore Perimeter Trail

    Find Isak Heartstone — the 15-ft wooden troll!
    Famous troll sculpture hidden in the woods near Breckenridge · The kids will love it
  • 🛍️

⛰️ Trip Reminders

Sunscreen!High altitude = stronger UV. Apply before St. Mary's Glacier and every outdoor day.
AltitudeDillon & Breckenridge are at 9,000+ ft. Drink extra water the first couple of days.
Picnic PlanningPack the St. Mary's Glacier picnic Monday morning before leaving the hotel.
Trail-a-Bike for NoraConsider renting one from Elevation Ski & Bike for the Blue River Bikeway ride.
Free ShuttleSummit Stage bus from Frisco → Breckenridge is free. Skip the Breck parking hassle!
Games to PackSeafarers Catan + Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza cards for Airbnb downtime.
Check-in TimeAirbnb check-in is 4:00 PM on Monday — head to Silverthorne fudge if you're early!
Marina ParkPlayground is right next to the Airbnb — easy afternoon activity for the kids.
