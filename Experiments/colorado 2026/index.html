28Tuesday

Breckenridge: Gondola, Troll & Marble Run

🛏️ Tonight: Dillon Airbnb · Night 2 of 3
  • 🚗
    Drive to Breckenridge
    ~20 min from Dillon · Pack a snack/breakfast for the gondola ride (Granola bars, Gatorade)
    • Park at the BreckConnect Gondola lot and use the Breckenridge FreeRide buses to get around
    • Pack the kids a change of clothes and crocs for Riverwalk fun
    • Pack a bag of golf balls for the giant marble run
  • 🚡
    Ride the BreckConnect Gondola (Free!)
    Ride up the mountain, explore, ride back down · Stunning views of the ski resort and valley
    • Gondola opens at 8:30am
    • There are no tickets to buy and no reservations to make. Just walk up to the Gondola and jump into the cab.
    • Snap a family photo in the gondola and at the top
  • 🛍️
    Explore Main Street Breckenridge and the Riverwalk
    • Check out the Blue River Plaza · Let kids play in the stream
    • Browse shops, grab snacks, chocolate, coffee
    • Leah could grab coffee at Cool River Coffee House while the kids play at the pond behind it
  • 🍽️
    Lunch on Main Street
    See options below
  • 🚌
    Take the Trolley to Isak Heartstone 🗺 Ice Rink Lot
    Trolley stations all along Main Street · Trailhead starts at the Ice Rink Lot
  • 🧌
    Find Isak Heartstone — the Breckenridge Troll 🗺 Map
    15-ft wooden troll sculpture on the Trollstigen Trail
  • 🏐
    Do the Ciügaboc Marble Run 🗺 Map
    A massive interactive wooden marble run built into the forest floor along the Illinois Creek Trail, between the Ice Arena and the troll · Bring golf balls or small wooden balls to roll through the run
  • 🌇
    Take the trolley back towards Main Street
    • The trolley will take you all the way back to the car at the Breck Connect Gondola if you're ready
    • Grab supper on Main Street
    • Grab ice cream or a treat on Main Street
    • Spend some more time at the Riverwalk

⛰️ Trip Reminders

Sunscreen!High altitude = stronger UV. Apply before St. Mary's Glacier and every outdoor day.
AltitudeDillon & Breckenridge are at 9,000+ ft. Drink extra water the first couple of days.
Picnic PlanningPack the St. Mary's Glacier picnic Monday morning before leaving the hotel.
Golf BallsDon't forget the golf balls for the giant marble run in Breckenridge.
Swim ShortsMaybe wear swim shorts for riverwalk in Breckenridge. Pool at the Airbnb too.
Games to PackSeafarers Catan + Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza cards for Airbnb downtime.
Target RunPick up a couple fun Minecraft Lego sets for Airbnb downtime.
Check-in TimeAirbnb check-in is 4:00 PM on Monday — head to Silverthorne fudge if you're early!
Marina ParkPlayground is right next to the Airbnb — easy afternoon activity for the kids.
